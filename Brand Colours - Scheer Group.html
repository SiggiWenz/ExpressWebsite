Brand Colours

White and black are two of the four core Scheer brand colours.

 

The order is immaterial because there is no hierarchy among the colours. What matters is that the colours support the message you want to convey, and the overall layout is not too colourful.

 

Progressive Scheer red is our signature and signal colour and completes the brand colours. In combination with black and white it gives our brand its characteristic look. The red colour is used solely for the signet, notifications, call-to-actions, and backgrounds. Scheer watermark red is meant solely for a watermark placement of the Y signet against red backgrounds.

 

The brand colours are equal in status and always appear in full tone or gradient bright. They must not be darkened or lightened.
